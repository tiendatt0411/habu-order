Privacy Policy – HaBu Studio

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information HaBu Studio, an internal tool of HÀBU ORDER, receives from TikTok and how it is handled. HaBu Studio is used only by the owner of the HÀBU ORDER TikTok account to upload the company's own videos to that account's TikTok drafts.

1. Information we receive from TikTok

When the account owner connects TikTok, HaBu Studio receives only what is needed to upload drafts: the account's basic profile (open_id, display name, avatar) and the access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok for the scopes user.info.basic and video.upload.

2. How we use it

This information is used only to show which TikTok account is connected and to upload HÀBU ORDER's own videos to that account's drafts. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

3. Where it is stored

Tokens and profile data are stored only on HÀBU ORDER's own computer, in a local settings file that is not shared online. HaBu Studio has no public servers and no user database.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share this information with anyone. Data is sent only to TikTok, through TikTok's official API, to perform the upload the account owner requested.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the TikTok account is connected. Disconnecting HaBu Studio, or revoking its permission in the TikTok app, stops all access, and the stored tokens are then deleted from the computer. To request deletion, contact anhemhabu@gmail.com.

6. Children

HaBu Studio is an internal business tool and is not directed to children.
